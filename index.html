Solving Underground Transit Panic & Cashless Exclusion

When subterranean transport networks drop offline, non-English speaking commuters face systemic friction, frozen mobile apps, and punitive fare-evasion fines. TransitGuard provides an offline-first cryptographic micro-token engine to guarantee gate clearance without cellular connections.

02

Proposed Solution & Technical Architecture

Offline-first, cryptographic pre-cached micro-UI engine

🔑

Pre-Cached Hardware Enclave

When the device has cellular connection above ground, the app fetches a pre-signed cryptographic seed stored locally inside the phone's Secure Enclave.

  • ✓ Elliptic-curve digital signatures (ECDSA)
  • ✓ Time-based rolling dynamic tokens
📲

Offline Optical Handshake

Generates a high-contrast dynamic QR code containing encrypted trip parameters that turnstile optical readers verify offline without calling central servers.

  • ✓ Zero server network dependency
  • ✓ Validates in under 200 milliseconds
🌐

Multilingual Emergency UI

In case of gate rejection, the screen displays a universal 3-color visual badge & native language card (Ukrainian, Portuguese, Chinese, Arabic, English).

  • ✓ Instant proof-of-valid-account card
  • ✓ Eliminates language-barrier distress

❓ Curiosity Questions for Real Data

Questions answerable only by interviewing transit authorities & migrant users in Dublin:

2. Fare Penalty Fine Demographics

📍 Where in Ireland: Transdev Luas Customer Care Office, Connolly Station Dublin.

3. Offline Hardware Capability

Do Dublin station turnstiles currently support offline cryptographic key storage on local reader heads?

📍 Where in Ireland: NTA Tech Integration Team / Dogpatch Labs FinTech Mentors.

🧪 €20 Smallest Test Protocol

Budget: €5.00 Spent

A 1-hour field validation experiment executed at Dublin transit hubs to measure real fear of offline fines versus ticket acceptance.

Step 1: Artifact Creation (€5.00)

Print 20 physical card slips with a custom mock dynamic QR code stating: "Offline Pre-Cleared Emergency Voucher".

Step 2: Field Execution (Connolly / Heuston Station)

Approach 15 commuters with phone on Airplane Mode. Ask: "If your transit app froze underground right now, would you feel safe showing this inspector backup slip, or do you expect a fine anyway?"

Step 3: Metrics to Track

Measure % who express fear of inspector fines vs. % who accept offline pre-authorization badges.
